About this model

Every building, road, shoreline and hill is placed from real map data on a single metric grid centred on Bahrain. Surfaces are generated on top of that geometry.

The model covers the whole kingdom, including the Hawar Islands and the King Fahd Causeway (62 × 105 km), with a strip of the Saudi and Qatari coasts for context.

Where building heights come from

Buildings with a height or floor count in OpenStreetMap use it. The rest take a satellite-derived height for that individual building from GlobalBuildingAtlas, snapped to whole storeys for low-rise; where it has no value, the 100 m block average from the Global Human Settlement Layer stands in. These estimates are typically within a storey or two, and run low on tall towers. Sheds and annexes get a small default.

People

Tags, hearts, tips, notes, questions and events are written by people using this page. Each is placed to the nearest 100 m and no address is stored. Anything reported by three people is hidden, and the page owner can remove any item.

Real

  • Building footprints and positions
  • Terrain, coastline, reclaimed land and islands
  • Roads, highways and the causeway
  • Ground colour and roof colour, from satellite imagery
  • Tree cover, placed where the imagery is green
  • Sun position for Bahrain at the March equinox
  • Governorate boundaries and place names

Generated

  • Window and facade patterns, wall paint, rooftop clutter
  • Tree shapes, wave motion, street and window lights
  • Traffic: cars drive in lane on the mapped roads, and their number and speed follow a typical Bahraini day from the clock (rush around 07:30 and 17:00). It is a simulation, not a live feed
  • Landmark models (the World Trade Center sails and turbine bridges, the Al Fateh Grand Mosque dome and minarets), built by hand on their real sites

Limits

  • Most footprints are traced by machine from satellite images, so some houses are merged with a neighbour or missing.
  • Ground imagery is 10 m per pixel, so the ground blurs when you are close.
  • Only two landmarks are modelled by hand. Every other tower, fort and mosque is a plain extrusion of its footprint.

Data

Buildings, roads and names: Overture Maps (OpenStreetMap contributors, Microsoft ML Building Footprints). Heights: GlobalBuildingAtlas LoD1 (Zhu et al., TUM, 2025); GHSL GHS-BUILT-H R2023A, European Commission JRC. Imagery: Sentinel-2 cloudless 2024 by EOX (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), modified Copernicus Sentinel data. Elevation: SRTM via Mapzen terrain tiles.

Simplified view: this browser has WebGL turned off, so buildings, roads and lighting are not shown. For the full 3D model, turn on “Use graphics acceleration when available” in Chrome Settings → System and relaunch.
